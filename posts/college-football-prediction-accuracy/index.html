How Accurate is the AP College Football Preseason Poll?

lowercaseword
Author

Graham Williams

Published

November 13, 2026

Towards the end of every summer as we ease closer and closer to football season, fans around the country want to know just how much potential their favorite teams have. Many fans turn to the AP preseason poll as a gauge for what they can expect once their team takes the field. 

The question is… does the AP preseason poll actually give us any insight into what the end of the season will look like? 

Using data from 2025, these three charts will help put into perspective how accurate the AP preseason poll really is.

The thing that pops out immediately in this bar chart showing the teams that dropped the most is USC. They started as #6 and dropped completely out of the top 25. It also shows that other teams have big changes as well with 11 teams all changing by at least 5 positions. On the other side of the spectrum, Missouri started the season unranked and went up to #8 at the end of the season.

Next, we have a scatterplot showing the change from preseason to postseason. This chart and the line on the chart shows that the trend is that the higher your preseason rank is, the higher your postseason rank is. However, as you can see throughout the chart, there is a significant amount of outliers that changed drastically from preseason to the final rank. 

While there is an upward trend, can we really trust the accuracy of this poll? That is up to you to decide.

Lastly, below is a slope chart showing where each team in the preseason top 10 started and finished. As you will see, there is a lot of variance in start and finish, however, USC is the only team from the top 10 that ended up unranked at the end of the year. 

Code 
top_10_journey <- cfb_polls |>
  filter(Preseason_Rank <= 10) |>
  select(Team, "1. Preseason" = Preseason_Rank, "2. Final" = Final_Rank) |>
  pivot_longer(
    cols = c("1. Preseason", "2. Final"),
    names_to = "Time",
    values_to = "Rank"
  )

So, is the AP preseason poll trustworthy? While it gives us good information on how these teams are looking to start the season off, it definitely shouldn’t be trusted as an accurate predictor for the end of the season.
